01Project types

Which mobile app for your business?

A mobile app is worth it when it offers what a website alone does poorly: the camera, notifications, a presence on the App Store and Google Play. Here are four typical cases.

  1. An app for your customers

    Appointment booking, ordering, tracking, subscriptions, personal accounts, notifications: your customers find you on their phone, in French and in Arabic.

    • Appointments
    • Ordering
    • Notifications
    • Customer account

  2. An app for your teams

    Site tracking, delivery rounds, readings, inventories: the team enters data in the field, photos included, even without a network, and entries sync when the connection returns.

    • Field work
    • Photos
    • Offline
    • Sync

  3. Your own app

    You have an idea to publish on the app stores: we scope it, design it, build it and put it online, as we did for Rihla.

    • Scoping
    • Mock-ups
    • Publication

  4. An extension of an existing service

    You already have a website or a platform: the mobile app extends it onto the phone, with the same accounts and the same data, as far as your system allows them to be connected.

    • Existing site
    • Accounts
    • Data

02Method

From idea to App Store.

Four steps: a written quote at the start, and an app ready for the App Store and Google Play at the end.

The four steps of a mobile app project

  1. Step 01 · free quote

    Scoping

    Together we list the screens, the features, the target platforms (iPhone, Android or both) and what must work without a network. You receive a detailed quote, free and with no obligation, with the price and the timeline.

  2. Step 02

    Mock-ups

    Every screen is drawn and approved with you before the first line of code, in both languages if needed: you see the app before it exists.

  3. Step 03

    Development and testing

    Test versions install on your phone at every step. You try them, adjust course, and stay in control of progress.

  4. Step 04

    Publication and follow-up

    We prepare and submit the app to the App Store and Google Play (store listings, screenshots, privacy policy), then hand over the source code and documentation. Fixes and improvements follow over time.

04Frequently asked questions

Mobile apps: your questions, our answers.

Do I need one app for iPhone and another for Android?

Not two separate projects: we build from a single code base, which gives an iPhone app and an Android app with the same features, evolving together. If a specific need called for something else, we would tell you at the scoping stage.

How much does a mobile app cost?

It depends on what it does: number of screens, user accounts, notifications, payment, offline mode, admin area, languages. We do not publish a single price, because no two apps are alike: we give you a detailed quote, free of charge, with the price and the timeline, in writing.

How long does it take to publish?

The timeline is in the quote, once the screens and features are listed. At the end of development, review by Apple and Google adds a step nobody controls: we build it into the schedule.

Can you publish the app on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes, we handle the requirements of both stores: the app listing, screenshots, privacy policy, declaration of the data collected and, when the app lets users create an account, account deletion from within the app. Apple and Google each require a paid developer account.

Will the app work without internet?

It can, if designed that way: useful data is kept on the phone, entries are saved, then synced when the network returns. We decide with you, at scoping, what must work offline.

Will the app be available in Arabic?

Yes: an interface in French, in Arabic or in both, with right-to-left reading handled from the mock-up stage. English can be added if your audience needs it.

Will I receive the source code?

Yes: at launch, we hand over the source code and the documentation.

How does the quote request work?

You write us a few lines: your business, what you want to build, the platforms you are targeting, your desired timeline. We reply within two business days, by phone or in writing, to understand your needs, then send a detailed quote, free and with no obligation, with the price and the timeline.
