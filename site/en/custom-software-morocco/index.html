02Method

From the way you work to the software.

We start by understanding your trade, deliver in stages, and the software then evolves with your business.

The four steps of a custom software project

  1. Step 01 · free quote

    Scoping

    We first look at how the work is done today: notebooks, spreadsheets, tools in place, documents to produce. We write down what the software must do and you receive a detailed quote, free and with no obligation, with the price and the timeline.

  2. Step 02

    Design

    Screens and documents (receipts, invoices, exports) are drawn and approved with you, and with the people who will use them every day.

  3. Step 03

    Staged development

    The software is delivered in stages to a test address: you try it with your own cases, and we adjust before going further.

  4. Step 04

    Go-live and follow-up

    Go-live, handover of the source code and documentation, then fixes and improvements: business software keeps pace with changes in your business.

04Frequently asked questions

Custom software: your questions, our answers.

Custom or off-the-shelf software: which should I choose?

Off-the-shelf software suits standard needs. Custom software is worth it when your trade has its own rules, when you work around a generic tool with spreadsheets, or when you want software whose source code and documentation you receive. We tell you frankly, at scoping, if existing software is enough.

Can you replace our Excel files or notebooks?

That is a possible starting point: the software takes the structure of your tables and adds accounts, access rights and documents. Importing your existing data is studied at scoping.

Does the software work without internet?

Where your business requires it (point of sale, construction sites, travel), it records operations on the device and syncs when the network returns. We specify with you, at scoping, what must remain available offline.

What documents can the software produce?

Receipts, invoices, accounting exports, closing statements: documents are defined with you, starting from those you already use.

Is my data protected?

We plan for encryption and backups, and we identify with you the obligations under law 09-08 on personal data (declaration to or authorisation from the CNDP, as the case may be). Access is limited by user and by role.

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on the number of modules, users and documents, the target devices, offline mode and links with your accounting. We give you a detailed quote, free of charge, with the price and the timeline, in writing.

Will I receive the source code?

Yes: at launch, we hand over the source code and the documentation.

How does the quote request work?

You write us a few lines: your business, what you want to build, the platforms you are targeting, your desired timeline. We reply within two business days, by phone or in writing, to understand your needs, then send a detailed quote, free and with no obligation, with the price and the timeline.
