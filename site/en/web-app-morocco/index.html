Which web application for your business?

A web application opens in the browser, with nothing to install, and can be added to the phone's home screen like an app. Here are its most common uses.

  1. Marketplace and matching platforms

    Buyers and sellers, patients and doctors, students and teachers: accounts, search, appointments or orders between two audiences, on a single platform.

    • Accounts
    • Search
    • Appointments
    • Messaging

  2. Customer portals

    Your customers check their files, invoices and progress, upload documents and write to you at any hour, instead of calling you.

    • Accounts
    • Documents
    • Tracking
    • Invoices

  3. Back offices and dashboards

    Behind the scenes: manage content, users and orders, track activity, export to accounting.

    • Administration
    • Statistics
    • Exports

  4. Websites and online shops

    A website presenting your business, or a shop with catalogue, cart, orders and a payment method suited to your customers.

    • Website

    • Cart
    • Orders

04Frequently asked questions

Web applications: your questions, our answers.

Web application, showcase website, online shop: what is the difference?

A website presents your business. An online shop sells: catalogue, cart, orders. A web application puts your users to work: accounts, data, rules, documents. We build all three; at scoping, we pick the simplest option that meets your need.

Can a web application replace a mobile app?

Often, to begin with: it can be added to the phone's home screen, loads quickly and can work offline. The app stores mainly bring visibility on the App Store and Google Play and access to certain device features. At scoping, we tell you whether a mobile app is worth the investment in your case.

How much does a web application cost?

It depends on what it does: number of pages and features, types of users, online payment, admin area, languages, links with your other tools. We give you a detailed quote, free of charge, with the price and the timeline, in writing.

Will my site rank well on Google?

We take care of the technical foundation: fast pages, titles and descriptions, sitemap, structured data. Rankings also depend on content and time: nobody can promise a position, and we will not.

Can you take over an existing site or application?

Yes: audit of what exists, fixes, modernisation or rebuild. That is the role of our consulting and takeover service.

Can my web application take payments?

The payment method (cash on delivery, bank transfer, online card payment) is one of the questions at scoping. We tell you what is feasible for your business, and at what cost, before you commit to anything.

Will I receive the source code?

Yes: at launch, we hand over the source code and the documentation.

How does the quote request work?

You write us a few lines: your business, what you want to build, the platforms you are targeting, your desired timeline. We reply within two business days, by phone or in writing, to understand your needs, then send a detailed quote, free and with no obligation, with the price and the timeline.
