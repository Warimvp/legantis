How we work

Four steps, from scoping to follow-up.

  1. Step 01 · free quote

    Scoping

    We listen to your needs and write them down: screens, rules, priorities. You receive a detailed quote, free and with no obligation.

  2. Step 02

    Design

    Mock-ups of every screen, in both languages if needed, approved with you before the first line of code.

  3. Step 03

    Development

    Regular deliveries to a test address: you try the application at every step and adjust course.

  4. Step 04

    Launch and follow-up

    Publication, handover of the source code and documentation, then fixes and improvements over time.

04Frequently asked questions

Before you write to us.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what it does: number of screens, user accounts, languages, target platforms. We do not publish a single price, because no two projects are alike: we give you a detailed quote, free of charge, with the price and the timeline, and you then decide, freely.

How quickly do you reply?

Within two business days: a first conversation, by phone or in writing, to understand your needs. The detailed quote follows, free and with no obligation.

How long does a project take?

The timeline is in the quote, once the screens and features are listed. During development, you try the application on a test address at every delivery.

Will I receive the source code?

Yes: at launch, we hand over the source code and the documentation.

What happens after launch?

The same team handles fixes and improvements to your application over time.

Do you work in Arabic?

Yes: an interface in French, in Arabic or in both, with right-to-left reading handled from the mock-up stage. English can be added if your audience needs it.
