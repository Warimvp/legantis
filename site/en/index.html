Services

What we build for you

What we do for our own products, we do for your business: a complete application, designed, built, published and maintained by the same team, from the brief to launch.

  1. Web applications

    Platforms, customer portals, dashboards, websites and online shops. Fast, installable on a phone like a native app, and able to work offline when field conditions require it.

    Platform · customer portal · back office · website · online shop

  2. Mobile applications

    iPhone and Android from a single code base, published on the App Store and Google Play: camera, notifications, sharing, offline use. We handle both stores' requirements.

    iOS · Android · App Store · Google Play

  3. Business software

    Point of sale, management, activity tracking, internal tools: software shaped for your trade, with your rules and your documents (receipts, invoices, accounting exports).

    Point of sale · management · field tracking · internal tools

  4. Consulting and takeovers

    Project scoping, architecture choices, security and quality audits, compliance with Morocco's data protection law 09-08, and taking over or modernising an application already in place.

    Scoping · architecture · security · compliance · takeover

How we work

  1. Scoping

    We listen to your needs and write them down: screens, rules, priorities. You receive a detailed quote.

  2. Design

    Mock-ups of every screen, in both languages if needed, approved with you before the first line of code.

  3. Development

    Regular deliveries to a test address: you try the application at every step and adjust course.

  4. Launch and follow-up

    Publication, handover of the source code and documentation, then fixes and improvements over time.

These services fall under the three activities recorded in the commercial register: software publishing, IT consulting and online sales.
