01Project types

Which website for your business?

A website presents your business and stays available at any hour. Depending on what you expect from it, your project falls into one of these four cases.

  1. Showcase site

    Present your company, your services, your work and your contact details on a few clear pages, with a simple way to reach you by phone or email.

    • Presentation
    • Services
    • Contact

  2. Catalogue site

    Let visitors browse your products or services with detailed pages, photos and categories, without online sales: they choose on the site, then call or write to you.

    • Catalogue
    • Detail pages
    • Categories

  3. Online shop

    A catalogue, a cart, orders and a payment method suited to your customers: cash on delivery, bank transfer or card, depending on what is feasible for your business.

    • Catalogue
    • Cart
    • Orders

  4. Redesign of an existing site

    Your current site is old, slow, hard to read on a phone or impossible to edit: we rethink it and rebuild it, keeping what works. For a prior opinion on what exists, see our consulting and takeover service.

    • Redesign
    • Mobile
    • Content carried over

02Method

From idea to live site.

A written quote at the start, pages approved before they are built, and the source code at the end.

The four steps of a website project

  1. Step 01 · free quote

    Scoping

    Together we define the purpose of the site, the pages, the languages and what visitors must be able to do: call you, request a quote, order. You receive a detailed quote, free and with no obligation, with the price and the timeline.

  2. Step 02

    Mock-ups

    Each page type is drawn for the phone as well as the computer, in both languages if needed, and approved with you before it is built.

  3. Step 03

    Development

    The site is built on a test address: you browse it as your visitors will and adjust course before launch.

  4. Step 04

    Launch and follow-up

    Publication of the site, handover of the source code and documentation, then fixes and improvements over time.

04Frequently asked questions

Website development: your questions, our answers.

Showcase site, online shop or web application: which one?

To present your business, a showcase site is enough. To sell online, you need a shop. If your users must log in, manage data or follow files, it is rather a web application. At scoping we choose the simplest solution that meets your need.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the languages, the features (catalogue, online ordering, admin area) and the amount of content to carry over. We do not publish a single price: we give you a detailed quote, free of charge, with the price and the timeline, in writing.

Will my site be in French and Arabic?

Yes, if your audience needs it: French, Arabic or both, with right-to-left reading handled from the mock-up. English can be added if needed.

Will my site appear on Google?

We prepare the ground: titles and descriptions specific to each page, sitemap, markup, fast pages. Being shown, then ranked well, also depends on the content, on links to your site and on time: we promise no position.

Can I sell online?

That is a scoping question: cash on delivery, bank transfer or online card payment depending on your business. We tell you what is feasible, and at what cost, before any commitment on your side.

Can you rebuild my current site?

Yes: we keep what works (content, structure) and rebuild the rest. If you first want an opinion on what exists, that is the role of our consulting and takeover service.

What happens to my site after launch?

We handle fixes and improvements over time. The source code and documentation are handed over at launch.

How does the quote request work?

You write a few lines: your business, the type of site you want, the languages, the timeline. We reply within two business days, by phone or in writing, then send a detailed quote, free and with no obligation, with the price and the timeline.
